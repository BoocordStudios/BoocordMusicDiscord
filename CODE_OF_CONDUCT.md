# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the technical facts.
- Respect differing viewpoints and accept useful feedback.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/BoocordStudios/BoocordMusicDiscord/security/advisories/new). Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical and will review reports fairly.
